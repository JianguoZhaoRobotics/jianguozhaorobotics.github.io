Courses

MECH 416: Introduction to Robotic Manipulators

Undergraduate · Fall 2026 · Online book

An introduction to robotic manipulators that combines theory with hands-on practice. Topics include robot actuators, rigid-body transformations, forward and inverse kinematics, velocity kinematics and the Jacobian, and trajectory generation. Each topic is implemented in Python, tested in MuJoCo simulation, and then run on real hardware: students build and program a planar 2R arm from smart servo motors and control the open-source SO-101 robot arm.

MECH 529: Advanced Mechanical Systems

Graduate · Fall 2018, Spring 2020, Spring 2021, Spring 2022, Fall 2025 · Interactive lab materials

Advanced topics in mechanical systems analysis, modeling, and design, including differential equations, control systems, trajectory optimization, and reinforcement learning applications to robotic systems. Students work through 10 programming labs in Python and Jupyter plus a capstone project on acrobot control.

MECH 564: Fundamentals for Robot Mechanics and Control

Graduate · Spring 2016–Spring 2025 · Interactive lab materials

Fundamentals of robot mechanics, kinematics, dynamics, and control for robotic applications.

MECH 630: Biologically Inspired Robotics

Graduate · Fall 2017, Fall 2019, Fall 2021

Bio-inspired robotic systems, including biomimetic design principles, locomotion mechanisms, and adaptive behaviors inspired by nature.

MECH 307: Mechatronics

Undergraduate · Spring 2017, Spring 2018, Spring 2019, Fall 2020, Fall 2023

Sensors; actuators; applied electronics; microcontroller interfacing and programming; open- and closed-loop control systems; mechatronic systems analysis and design.
